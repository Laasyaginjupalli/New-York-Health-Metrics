# New York Health Metrics – Business Analytics

## Project Overview
This project analyses New York's health and lifestyle metrics by benchmarking it against other major global cities. The objective is to identify factors affecting New York's health rankings and explore data-driven recommendations to improve public health and quality of life.

## Key Areas of Analysis

**1. Data Exploration & Cleaning**
- Explored health and lifestyle indicators, including obesity rates, life expectancy, air pollution, gym membership costs, outdoor activities, and working hours.
- Cleaned the dataset by converting percentage and currency values into numerical formats.
- Selected relevant metrics and cities for comparative analysis.

**2. Comparative & Descriptive Analysis**
- Benchmarked New York against cities including Los Angeles, San Francisco, Chicago, Sydney, Amsterdam, and others.
- Examined city health rankings and lifestyle differences.
- Analysed outdoor activity availability, working hours, life expectancy, and takeaway food trends.

**3. Data Visualisation & Insights**
- Created visualisations to identify differences in health and lifestyle indicators across cities.
- Identified areas where New York could improve, particularly outdoor recreation opportunities and lifestyle-related health factors.
- Interpreted patterns connecting working hours, life expectancy, and overall well-being.

**4. Recommendations & Business Insights**
- Proposed increasing access to parks, cycling paths, and recreational spaces.
- Recommended initiatives to improve work-life balance and encourage healthier lifestyles.
- Suggested stronger pollution management and public health awareness campaigns.

## Key Takeaways
The analysis highlighted the importance of lifestyle affordability, outdoor recreation, environmental quality, and work-life balance in understanding city health metrics. It demonstrated how data analytics and comparative benchmarking can support informed policy recommendations and improve urban quality of life.
